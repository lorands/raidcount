= Scoring a raid

A raid scores in three ways:

Touch points:: One point for every defender the raider touches
and who is then out.
Bonus point:: One point when the raider crosses the bonus line with at least six defenders on court.
Tackle point:: One point to the defending team when they stop the raider.

[source,shell]
----
raidcount raid --team A --touch 2 --bonus
----

Record every raid in the order it happened: the raid log is the match record.
